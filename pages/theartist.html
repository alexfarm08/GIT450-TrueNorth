Where It All Started

Decades ago, I picked up a camera for the first time and fell in love. Every day, I would go hiking along the lakes, creeks, and forests of California’s San Bernardino Mountains, where I grew up. I would take pictures of water flowing over the rocks in the streams by my house or stunning views of the Inland Empire from “The Rim,” a cliffside highway that ran through town. Photography became my sport. I watched videos and learned from the masters, then practiced what I had learned until I felt I had perfected it. Sometimes, I returned to the same locations to test new compositions, exposure times, or lighting conditions until I got it just right. I printed, matted, and submitted “Pristine” to the Mountain Artist’s Guild and won first place in a student art competition. This validated and cemented that landscape photography was something I truly enjoyed and was good at.

Baxter Lakin

A Travel Partner

I met my now dog, Baxter, and from the very beginning, we bonded over our shared spirit of adventure and love for the great outdoors. We would get in the car as often as we could and drive for hours until we got somewhere new to explore, never taking the same road twice. That same spirit has guided us on countless journeys together. We've traveled to 3 countries and 27 states, driven over 250,000 miles, and taken over a million photos, and counting.

Come Along With Us

If you're not already, consider taking a moment to follow our journey on Instagram @truenorth, where we share stories about our adventures and the beautiful public lands Baxter and I have enjoyed exploring. By this point, I think we can call ourselves experts. So, if you ever have questions about national parks, hike recommendations, the great outdoors, or if you're just traveling somewhere and would like some recommendations, send us a message or leave a comment ! We'd love to connect!
